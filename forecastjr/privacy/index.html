Forecast JR Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Forecast JR is a free weather app made by Jeff Rosemeyer, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

Forecast JR has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting, and there is no Forecast JR server. The developer never receives your location or any other information about you. The app sends your location only to the U.S. government weather services that provide the forecast, and keeps everything else on your device.

Location

If you allow it, Forecast JR uses your device's approximate location to show the weather where you are. You can also deny location access and search for places instead.

To get weather for your location, or for a place you search for or save, the app sends that place's coordinates directly from your device to these services:

  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov): forecasts, current conditions, alerts and forecast discussions
  • U.S. Naval Observatory (aa.usno.navy.mil): sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset times

The app also sends the identifiers of nearby weather stations, not your coordinates, to the Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov) for the latest airport weather reports. Radar, hazard maps, severe weather outlooks and space weather are downloaded without any location.

These are U.S. government (NOAA and U.S. Navy) services. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and the app's name with each request, and their own privacy policies apply. Forecast JR does not send them anything else about you.

Alert notifications and the widget

If you turn on alert notifications, Forecast JR checks the National Weather Service for new alerts in the background, about every 15 minutes. If you add the home screen widget, it refreshes the forecast in the background about every 30 minutes. Both use the last location the app found with your device's location, which it saves on your device. The app does not access your location in the background.

Place search

Place search works entirely on your device, using a built-in list of places from the U.S. Census Bureau and ZIP codes from GeoNames. What you type into the search box is never sent anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Forecast JR stores your saved and recent places, your settings, the location used for alert checks, and recently downloaded weather data so it can show it offline. This stays on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may include it in your device's backup to your Google account. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Sharing

Forecast JR does not sell, rent or share your information. When you tap Share, the app hands the forecast text to the app you choose, and nothing else.

Children

Forecast JR is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date, and in the app.
